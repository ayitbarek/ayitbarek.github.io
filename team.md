### Principal Investigator
**Dr. Alex Yitbarek**  
Assistant Professor, Department of Animal and Food Sciences  
University of Delaware  

![Alex Yitbarek](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=view&id=1UiUt53gFq611qQHhciuX7ySOlf8JyI5n)
